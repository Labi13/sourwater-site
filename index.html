      Questions

      What does SOURwater do?

      SOURwater is a branding studio in Athens and New York. We build brand systems, websites made for Google and AI search, social content engines and AI brand films.

      What is GEO, and how is it different from SEO?

      SEO helps a page rank in search results. GEO (generative engine optimisation) makes a page clear and structured enough to be quoted in AI answers such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews. We build for both.

      Do you work in Greek and English?

      Yes. Every site, campaign and content system can be built in Greek and English, each written natively rather than translated.

      How much does a project cost?
